What Is a QR Code?

A Quick Response (QR) code is a two-dimensional matrix barcode readable by any smartphone camera. It stores website URLs, WiFi credentials, vCard contacts, menus, PDFs, or payment URIs for instant offline-to-online access.

How to Create a QR Code Online

Open the , choose from 27 QR types, enter your link or data, verify the live preview, and download your QR code in PNG, SVG, JPG, or PDF.

How to Customize a QR Code

Use QRora's to pick brand HEX/RGB/HSL colors, multi-stop gradients, rounded or diamond dots, custom finder eyes, and call-to-action frames.

How to Add a Logo to a QR Code

Upload a PNG or SVG brand mark in the Logo tab. QRora automatically activates Error Correction Level H (30% recovery) and clean background padding so your branded QR code scans reliably.

How to Create a WiFi QR Code

Select the , enter your SSID, encryption (WPA/WEP/open), and password. Guests scan once to join automatically.

How to Create a PDF QR Code

Paste any publicly accessible HTTPS PDF link into the to share brochures, restaurant menus, or product manuals with zero broken local paths.

How to Make QR Code Stickers

Launch the , pick a Scan Me, Our Menu, Pay Here, or Free WiFi layout, customize typography and borders, and export 300 DPI printable files.

Static vs. Dynamic QR Codes

Static QR codes encode data directly and work free forever. Dynamic QR codes use a Supabase redirect endpoint so you can edit destinations, add passwords, set expiry dates, and record real scan analytics.

Privacy Policy

Static QR codes generated in QRora are encoded directly in your browser. We do not transmit static QR payloads to third-party servers.

When you configure Supabase for user accounts, file storage, or dynamic QR redirects, Row Level Security (RLS) ensures only the authenticated owner can read or modify private QR records.
